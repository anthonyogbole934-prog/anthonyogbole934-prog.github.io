SCRIPT
[on-screen text: Remote PM Meeting Struggles]
If you are a remote project manager juggling five to ten client calls a week, minutes add up fast. When a 45-minute kickoff gets cut short, it is a total workflow killer. 

[on-screen text: 1. Fathom]
First up is Fathom. It is a favorite for solo users because it offers free, unlimited transcription and recording. You can click to highlight key moments, and it builds a summary instantly. 

[on-screen text: 2. Fireflies.ai]
Next is Fireflies.ai. Its AI assistant 'Fred' joins your calls to take notes. It has a great free tier with robust search features so you can easily find past action items or speaker names. 

[on-screen text: 3. Krisp]
Then we have Krisp. Known for blocking background noise, it now offers meeting notes too. It ensures your audio is crystal clear before transcribing. 

[on-screen text: 4. Avoma]
Finally, Avoma is an end-to-end assistant. It handles agenda planning, smart categorization, and deep CRM tracking, though the free tier is quite limited. 

[on-screen text: Choose Your Tool]
Choose Fathom for unlimited individual recording, Fireflies for searchable archives, Krisp for noise cancellation, or Avoma for lifecycle management!
